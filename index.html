Packet Switching Lab

Many senders share one router link. Packets that cannot be sent right away wait in a buffer (queue). When the buffer is full, new packets are dropped. Change the settings and watch what happens.

Router buffer (filled boxes are packets waiting):

Try: arrivals lower than link speed (little waiting), equal (queue builds, delay rises), higher (buffer fills, packets are lost). This is the packet-switching idea behind delay and loss in Unit 1.
